BTQCBrain Tumor Quantification & Confidence

Understanding Brain Tumors Through Uncertainty-Aware Imaging

A research platform for glioma segmentation on multi-institution MRI. BTQC wraps nnU-Net v2, measures where a segmentation is uncertain, and tests how it holds up at hospitals it has never seen, using FeTS 2022 data.

Research design

Transparent by construction

Every number in this interface comes from a script and a config file in the repository. Where no result exists, the interface says so rather than filling the gap.

01 · DATA

Multi-institution MRI

FeTS 2022 glioma scans with four sequences, converted to canonical RAS. A planted voxel round-trips through Python and the browser.

02 · SEGMENTATION

nnU-Net v2

Region-based whole tumor, tumor core and enhancing tumor, trained with a site-held-out fold: the seven institutions of group mid never seen in training (laptop pilot, 10 epochs).

03 · UNCERTAINTY

Boundary-band score

Entropy of the ensemble and flip-TTA mean, averaged in a 3-voxel band around the predicted tumor boundary.

04 · REVIEW GATE

One advisory rule

flag = score > review limit, and nothing else. A missing score is flagged. The limit is fixed before held-out evaluation.

What this prototype is not

  • Not a medical device, and not for diagnosis or treatment decisions.
  • The trust flag is advisory. It depends only on an uncertainty score and a review limit.
  • It does not estimate tumor grade or stage.
  • A laptop pilot: one held-out site group, 10 training epochs, a sampled test set. The 219 unlabeled validation scans still show rule-based EXAMPLE outlines.
